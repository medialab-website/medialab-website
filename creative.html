Commercial Video Projects

Promotions & Marketing

A compelling 30-second to 1-minute promotional video for a local business to use on social media or their website.

Baseline Estimate: Around $1000 for simple shoots, increasing depending on complexity, talent, and licensing.

Full-Day Production: Projects requiring a full 8-hour day of filming carry a base rate of $2,500 per day.

Music Videos

We work closely with artists to bring their sonic vision to the screen, ranging from gritty performance pieces to narrative shorts.

Simple Performance: Musicians can take advantage of straightforward, one-shot style performance videos for as low as $500.

Narrative/Complex Production: Artists can expect to spend a base rate of about $2,500 for complex shoots involving multiple locations and heavy pre-production planning.

Event Coverage

From corporate conferences and grand openings to live concerts and festivals. This category relies on too many factors to state a standard package price upfront.

Full-Day Coverage: Any event shoot that requires a full 8-hour day of on-location filming starts at a base rate of $2,500.
